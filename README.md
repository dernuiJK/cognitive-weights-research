# Cognitive Weights

## Independent research overview

Cognitive Weights is an ongoing, self-funded experimental research project led by the independent researcher behind [@dernuiJK](https://github.com/dernuiJK). This repository provides a high-level public introduction, not a release of implementation code or model weights.

### Research interests

- Persistent cognitive states: how learned information can remain available across interactions.
- Selective revision: how new evidence can change affected beliefs while preserving unrelated knowledge.
- Learning-to-action connections: how learned cognitive states can be used in behavior and revised through feedback.
- Reusable computational components: how narrowly tested components might contribute to larger learning systems.

These are research questions, not claims of completed general capabilities.

### Researcher role and AI assistance

I formulate the research questions, develop conceptual hypotheses, and oversee research direction. AI coding assistants support implementation, testing, and documentation. Their involvement does not constitute independent scientific validation.

### Status and evidence boundaries

The project remains under investigation. This introductory page does not present experimental results or provide evidence for general intelligence, unrestricted continual learning, or open-world reliability. Findings should be assessed through their corresponding experimental methods, controls, and limitations. This page is not a peer-reviewed publication.

### Public scope

Only a research overview is published here. Source code, model weights, detailed algorithms, and raw experimental records are not included. This independent project does not claim institutional affiliation or endorsement by an AI provider.

## 中文研究简介

### 研究方向

- 持续认知状态：学习获得的信息如何在不同交互之间持续可用。
- 选择性修订：新证据如何修改受影响的认识，同时保留无关知识。
- 学习与行为连接：学习形成的认知状态如何用于行为，并通过反馈进一步修订。
- 可复用计算构件：经过窄局部验证的构件如何为更大的学习系统提供支持。

这些是研究问题，不代表相应通用能力已经实现。
